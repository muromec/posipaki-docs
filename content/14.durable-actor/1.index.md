# A durable actor

An actor whose state is the fold of a durable record log, and whose only words are whether it took a request.

```ts
type FilterResult<P, E = string> = { pass: P } | { refuse: E };

interface RecordSpec<S, R, Q, P = Q, E = string> {
  filter(state: S, request: Q): FilterResult<P, E>; // may it go on, and in what shape
  map(state: S, passed: P): R;                      // the record it becomes
  reduce(state: S | null, record: R): S;            // one record onto the fold
  diff(before: S | null, after: S): R;              // the way back
}
```

- **filter gates map.** A `refuse` writes nothing; a `pass` means `map` is called exactly once.
- **map is pure.** Same state, same passed request, same record — twice. That is why the clock lives in `filter`:
  `P` is what a filter passes on, so a spec that stamps a moment says so and its `map` takes it.
- **reduce is the fold.** A journal in, the state out; `reduce(null, r)` is the first step.
- **diff inverts reduce.** `reduce(before, diff(before, after))` is `after`, for every adjacent pair — which
  forces the state to remember what its records said.

## Live demo

Three boards. The first owns the log: it filters what it is asked and writes the record. The second follows it
and defers: it says nothing until the record has come back. The third follows the second and guesses: it shows
the change at once, and if the authority refuses the guess it folds back and says so.

Pick a figure, then a box. Click a filled box to lift its figure. Two figures of the same shape and colour on one
board clear it. Cut a channel and watch everything behind it stop.

::demo{durable}

The widget runs a copy of the pattern (`src/durable.js` in this tree); the shipped implementation is
`posipaki-durable-actor`.
